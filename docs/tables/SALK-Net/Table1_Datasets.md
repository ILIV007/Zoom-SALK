# Table 1 — Information of COD Datasets

**Source:** SALK-Net paper, Page 10 (Table 1)

| Dataset | Published | Train | Test |
|---------|-----------|-------|------|
| CHAMELEON | Unpublished | – | 76 |
| CAMO | CVIU | 1000 | 250 |
| COD10K | TPAMI | 3040 | 2026 |
| NC4K | CVPR | – | 4121 |

**Notes (extracted from the paper):**
- The training set is composed of **CAMO + COD10K** (1000 + 3040 = 4040 training images).
- CHAMELEON and NC4K are used **only for testing** (untrained datasets) to evaluate generalization.
- COD10K is the largest single test set (2026 images) and the primary benchmark reported in ablation studies.
- NC4K (4121 test images) is the largest test set overall.
