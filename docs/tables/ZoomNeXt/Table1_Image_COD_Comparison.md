**Key observations:**
- ZoomNeXt consistently outperforms all competitors across all backbones and datasets.
- RN50_ZoomNeXt (28.458M params, 384²): COD10K Sm 0.861, Fω 0.768, MAE 0.026 — the lightest variant.
- PVTv2-B5_ZoomNeXt (84.774M): best overall accuracy — COD10K Sm 0.898, Fω 0.827, MAE 0.018.
- ZoomNeXt with RN50 beats ZoomNet (the conference version) on every dataset.
